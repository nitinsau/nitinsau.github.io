Topics in Quantum Computing (CS6340)

Lectures     Monday (2:30pm--3:55pm) and Thursday (4:00pm--5:25pm)
Venue CS-LH-1

Syllabus

  • Introduction to Quantum Computing.
  • Bounded-depth quantum circuits.
  • Quantum algorithms using random walks, Hamiltonian Simulation and the HHL algorithm.
  • Other advanced topics if time permits...

Evaluations
